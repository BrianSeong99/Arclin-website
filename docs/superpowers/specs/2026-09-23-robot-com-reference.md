# robot.com homepage: reference specification for the Arclin v3 build

Source: five Playwright capture passes against `https://www.robot.com/` on 2026-09-23 (structure, motion, interactions, responsive, assets-type) plus a re-measurement pass. All numbers are computed style / `getBoundingClientRect` values at 1440x900 unless a viewport is stated. Where a first-pass claim was disputed by the re-measurement, the re-measured value is used and the dispute is noted. Companion data file: `robot-com-reference.json` (band table, motion table, type map, checklist).

Kurogane token names are those in `app/globals.css` on branch `v3-site` (`--surface-page`, `--surface-raised`, `--surface-sunken`, `--surface-brand`, `--hairline`, `--border-strong`, `--ink`, `--ink-muted`, `--ink-subtle`, `--on-brand`, `--on-brand-muted`, `--highlight`, `--highlight-edge`, `--on-highlight`, `--focus-ring`, `--focus-ring-inverse`, `--radius-sm/md/lg/pill`, `--dur-enter`, `--dur-slow`, `--ease-enter`).

## 1. What the page is

robot.com's homepage is a single-column stack of rounded slabs on a white body. Every slab is inset 5px from each viewport edge (`.Container_container { margin: 0 auto; width: calc(100% - 1rem); max-width: 1928px }`, rendered 1430px wide at x=5 at 1440; 758 at 768; 380 at 390), carries a 24px radius (26px on the two ScrollText slabs and the Formic slab), and is separated from the next by a 4px white seam produced by `padding-top: 4px` on 7 of the 9 in-flow `<section>`s (the partner-logo section and the CMS html-block section have 0; the announcement wrapper has `margin-top: 5px`). Inside slabs the grid is 24 columns of 55.75px with a 4px gap (= 1430); cards span 6 (354.5px) or 12 (713px). Content padding inside slabs is 24px, so text starts at x=29 in 10 of 13 bands. `html` is 10px (`rem` = 10px); above 1492px the root font-size becomes `calc(10 * 100vw / 1492)` so the whole page scales fluidly. The page is 7674px tall at 1440x900, 7181 at 768x1024 and 9572 at 390x844 (first pass read 9578; 6px lazy-media variance). There is no horizontal overflow at any of the three viewports. Motion is driven by Lenis 1.0.42 (smooth wheel, `duration: 1.2`, `lerp: false`, expo-out easing), GSAP 3.12.7 + ScrollTrigger (two word-opacity scrubs, one pin, two footer callbacks, nav-height and accordion tweens), GSAP SplitText 3.13 (splits `.animated-title` headings into `.line > .word`) with `react-intersection-observer` (`threshold: 0.5`, `triggerOnce`) adding `.is-in`, and plain CSS transitions for every hover. framer-motion is bundled and used only for the accordion image crossfade. Font is Yellix 400/500/600 self-hosted (`font-display: swap`), `font-feature-settings: "ss01"` globally. No box-shadow anywhere on the page; the only borders are 1px on outline pills and the nav toggle.

## 3. Components

### 3.1 Nav pill

Desktop (≥768): `header` fixed top 0, width 100%, `padding: 16px 0`, z-index 11, `pointer-events: none` (children re-enable). ## 7. Open questions

1. Band order 5/6. The v3 brief lists "5 the robot (product block), 6 scale band (surface-brand)"; robot.com's order is statement slab (dark) then product band (yellow). This spec maps the robot to the yellow product band and the scale figures to the dark statement slab because the brief's "(surface-brand)" on band 6 matches the dark slab exactly. That swaps bands 5 and 6 relative to the brief's numbering. The alternative (robot in the dark slab, scale figures in the yellow band recoloured to brand) needs a decision.
2. Closing CTA surface. robot.com's CTA slab is `--highlight` (#fff65d); the brief says band 11 is `--surface-brand`. The inline pill inverts accordingly (`--highlight`/`--on-highlight` on brand). Confirm.
3. Content gaps. Every fact in bands 4 (trusted by), 5/6 (scale figures, robot specs), 7 (founder quote), 8 (safety sentences) and the second sentence of each market row is a `[GAP]` in `lib/i18n/messages/*.ts`. Per the brief, gapped bands are cut, which would leave bands 1, 2, 3, 9 (titles only), 10, 11, 12. Decide before build which bands ship.
4. Video assets. Bands 3 and 10 need an mp4 (robot.com: 1920x1080, 37.93s hero; Bunny CDN multi-bitrate for the lightbox). No Arclin video exists (`home.hero.videoLabel` is a label only). Until one exists, the hero needs a poster-only fallback measured the same way (1430x890 cover).
5. Second product card. The product band has two 713² cut-out cards (noid, dog). Arclin has one robot (Mimamori). Options: one card spanning 24 columns, or Mimamori + CareOS. Unmeasured either way.
6. Tonal inversion of cards. robot.com cards (#f8f6f3) are darker than its white page; Kurogane `--surface-raised` (#ffffff) is lighter than `--surface-page` (#fbf7ef). Mapping cards → `--surface-raised` reverses the relationship; mapping them → `--surface-sunken` (#f2ecdf) preserves it but reads darker. Decide.
7. Radius 24/26 vs Kurogane `--radius-lg: 20px`. Matching robot.com needs `--radius-xl: 24px` (and 26 collapsed to 24). The design spec forbids hard-coded values in components, so the token must be added.
8. Motion tokens. Kurogane's 240/320ms envelope does not contain any measured robot.com duration (300/400/450/600/1000/1200ms). Section 5 lists the tokens to add; the 09-22 design spec ("240ms, one ease", "accordion 240ms") must be amended or the "same as robot.com" requirement relaxed.
9. Glass header. `backdrop-filter: blur(26px)` over rgba(255,255,255,.1) / rgba(38,38,38,.075) has no token. Add `--glass-on-brand`/`--glass-on-page` or specify literals in `Nav`.
10. Header submenu. robot.com's menu is a 3-tile + carousel dropdown that opens on hover with no aria-expanded; Arclin's brief is "floating nav pill: wordmark, 4 links, locale toggle, CTA pill" with links visible. The open/close tween (1000ms expo.out height) only applies if Arclin keeps a dropdown at 390. Decide whether the mobile menu reuses M15–M19.
11. Fluid root scale. robot.com scales the whole page above 1492px (`calc(10*100vw/1492)`). Arclin's max-width container (`84rem`) and px type do not. Decide whether to match above 1492.
12. 390 measurements not taken: section paddings, the CTA `h3` size (only the 42px container was read), accordion inner timings at 768, footer link roll-over at 390. Band tops at 390 are contiguous, so the 4px seam is inferred, not read.
13. Interlude parallax driver. Measured as inline `translate3d` from an unidentified scroll listener (not ScrollTrigger, not framer-motion). The exact mapping (±3rem over one viewport height) is reconstructed from 6 samples; edge clamping at −30px is observed far below the band.
