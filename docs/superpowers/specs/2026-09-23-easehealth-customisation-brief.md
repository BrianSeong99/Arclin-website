# easehealth.com: customisation brief for the Arclin v3 homepage

Purpose: the approved homepage is a 1:1 structural and motion copy of robot.com built on Kurogane tokens (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-23-robot-com-reference.md`, thirteen bands in `app/[locale]/page.tsx`). The instruction is "customize it a little bit, borrow some inspirations from easehealth.com". This brief measures what easehealth.com does, ranks what is worth borrowing, and says what would break either the robot.com skeleton or a Kurogane rule. The skeleton stays robot.com: what moves is texture, tone, component detail and at most two band-level ideas.

## 1. What easehealth.com is

easehealth.com is a Webflow-exported Next.js marketing site for a behavioural-health EHR/CRM/RCM product: a 12616 px single-colour page (body `#fffefc`, no slabs, no seams) whose twelve bands are centred containers (1370 px "large" or 1042 px "medium", 35.2 px page gutter, `html` at 88% so 1 rem = 14.08 px) filled with rounded cards in three tints of one green (`#e1f4df`, `#cfe7d3`, `#b1dbb8`) plus a dark forest (`#0f3e17`) used for both text and every button. Radius is 14.08 px on 36 elements, 999 px on the 12 feature chips and their 12 dots, 7.04 px on the seven eyebrow tags and the four dropdown elements; there are no shadows anywhere and the only border on the page is the 1 px forest line on the five `.button`s. Headings are a light serif (Faire Octave 300, 73.9/56.3/40.5 px at 1.05/1.2/1.35 leading and -0.03/-0.03/-0.01 em tracking) over a small sans body (Suisse Intl 400 at 14.08 px, 1.5 leading); the tone is quiet, declarative, first-person-plural product copy, with numbers set large and light (56.3 px Suisse 300). Motion is slow and layered: Lenis smooth scroll (lerp 0.1), cards that wipe in from a clipped corner over 1500 ms, headings that rise 30 px over 1000 ms after a 300 ms delay, a word ticker that advances every 1.5 s, one pinned "problem to solution" band that swaps its background to sage over 800 ms and draws seven lines longer as you scroll, one horizontally pinned product carousel, and thirteen Lotties (UI mock-ups with raster avatars in the hero and product cards, line icons and line diagrams elsewhere). Hover is nearly silent: buttons darken to `#0c2f10` over 300 ms, links dim to 0.6, cards do nothing. Below 992 px it becomes a static page: every card reveal and heading entrance is skipped except the hero, and two marquees run (logos at 47 px/s, compliance items at 42 px/s).

Against robot.com, which we keep: robot.com is a stack of full-width slabs inset 5 px with 4 px seams, 24 px radii, a 24-column grid, one bold sans (Yellix 600) for every heading, black/yellow/white surfaces, a floating glass nav pill that never hides, 400 ms line reveals on headings and no card entrances, word-opacity scrubs and a pinned dot-mask product band. easehealth.com is the tonal opposite on the same bones: serif over sans instead of one heavy sans, tinted cards floating on one page colour instead of edge-to-edge slabs, 14 px radii instead of 24, slower and softer entrances, a nav that hides, and diagrams drawn as thin lines instead of dot matrices. Its register (light, unhurried, clinical-calm) is closer to Kurogane's than robot.com's is, which is why it is worth mining for texture even though its skeleton is not worth adopting.

## 3. Ranked borrow list

Costs: S = under two hours in one file, M = half a day across two to four files, L = a day or more or a new asset. "Fidelity" means the robot.com 1:1 requirement and its verification IDs in the reference spec.

1. Serif display for the band headings (site-wide; bands 4, 6, 8, 9, 11). What changes: the Chillax 41/600 card, accordion and CTA titles (`t-title-l` with inline `cardTitleType` in `audience-rows.tsx`, `titleStyle` in `product-band.tsx`, `.mkt__title` in `markets-accordion.tsx`, the `clamp` in `trusted-by.tsx`, `CTA_TYPE` 38/1.1 in `closing-cta.tsx`) move to `.t-display-m` (Italiana 40 px / 1.15 / +0.015 em), the class the hero and the interlude already use for `en`; JA and ZH keep `.t-jp-display` and the Noto equivalent as `interlude.tsx` already branches. Ease's measure: one light serif at 56.32 / 1.2 / -0.03 em for every band h2 and 40.48 / 1.35 for every h3, sans reserved for body, labels and numerals. Why it earns its place: it is the single cheapest thing that moves the page from "robotics start-up" to "care provider"; a light serif reads as institutional calm to procurement and as warmth to a family, and it is what Kurogane's own type rules describe (display for hero lines and editorial subheads, Chillax for the functional rest). Cost: M (five files plus a check that `reveal-heading.tsx`'s line wrappers accept the 1.15 leading). Conflicts: fidelity, since robot.com sets every heading in one 600 sans and spec section 4 asks for a Chillax `t-title-xl` (V-checks on heading metrics will need re-basing); Kurogane, since Italiana may never go below 40 px, so the 26 px mobile size in the spec's type table cannot be used: hold 40 px at 390 (as `hero.tsx` does) or keep Chillax below 768 and accept a family change across the breakpoint.

2. Sentence-case eyebrow chip instead of the uppercase overline (site-wide; bands 4, 6, 9 today). What changes: `components/site/section.tsx`'s `Eyebrow` (`t-overline`, 12 px uppercase 0.22 em) and the inline `t-overline` in `trusted-by.tsx` become a `.t-label` (14/600) chip, padding `--space-2` `--space-3`, `--radius-pill`, background `--surface-sunken` with text `--ink-muted` on page bands and `rgba` of `--on-brand` at 10% with `--on-brand-muted` text on brand slabs, sitting `--space-6` (24 px) above the heading; the same component fronts the robot eyebrow in `product-band.tsx` and the markets eyebrow in `markets-accordion.tsx`. Ease's measure: 17.6/400 chip, padding 10.56, radius 7.04, 28 px above the h2, seven per page, no uppercase anywhere. Why: Kurogane forbids uppercase and emoji as warmth and asks for sentence case on chips; tracked capitals are the hardest register for an eighty-year-old reader and the chip is the same information at 14 px in a friendlier shape. Cost: S (one component, three call sites). Conflicts: none with fidelity (robot.com has no eyebrows; the overline was already an Arclin addition); Kurogane reserves `overline` for exactly this use, so the brand book's overline goes unused on the homepage, a recorded deviation rather than a rule break. Do not colour the chip `--highlight`: the site already spends its two highlight bands.

## 4. Do not borrow

1. The auto-advancing word ticker (item 7): Kurogane motion, "nothing auto-advances while someone may still be reading it", and its 1000 + 500 ms cadence sits outside the 200 to 320 ms envelope.

2. The mobile logo and compliance marquees (item 10): the same rule, plus fidelity (robot.com's partner grid is static, spec section 2 row 4 "No marquee").

3. The three green tints, the slate tag colour and the mulberry check tiles: Kurogane colour is two colours plus `--highlight`; surfaces are page, raised, sunken, brand and highlight only; "if a photograph needs a colour wash it is the wrong photograph" applies to cards too. The nearest legitimate quiet card is `--surface-sunken` with no hairline, which the reference spec already lists as open question 6.

4. Ease's entrance timings and the 30 px rise (items 5 and 6): 1500 / 1150 / 1000 ms are four to five times Kurogane's ceiling, the 0.9 to 1 scale reads as a spring, and the animate-in system ignores reduced motion. Move 4 borrows the shape only.

5. The pinned problem-to-solution band and its sage background swap (item 8): a new band on a page whose skeleton already pins the product band; "a little bit" excludes new IA, and a full-panel colour swap is a colour wash.
